Delete your Pacekin account

This page deletes your Pacekin account and personal data. You can also delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete account).

What will be deleted

  • Your profile, username, bio, profile photos and chat photos
  • All your runs, GPS routes, streaks, badges and personal records
  • Your event registrations, group memberships and friend connections
  • Your messages (the text is removed from your profile; other people's copies of group messages may remain without your name)
  • Your notifications, device tokens and AI Coach data
  • Your sign-in

This cannot be undone. Groups you own will be closed. Some safety records are kept for up to 12 months, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Sign in with Google to confirm it's you. You'll be asked to sign in again before deleting.
