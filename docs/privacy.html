Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Pacekin ("we", "us") is a running community app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your choices. Please read it together with our Terms.

1. Who we are

Pacekin is operated by [YOUR LEGAL NAME / COMPANY], [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. Contact: support@yourdomain.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address and Google account identifier, received when you sign in with Google. We never receive your Google password.
  • Profile data: username, city, date of birth (used to check our minimum age and to show age to event organisers), gender (optional), bio, running experience, preferred distances, preferred times and running goal.
  • Profile photo: only if you upload one. Photo metadata such as location (EXIF) is removed when we store it.
  • Running activity: start and end times, distance, duration, pace, and the GPS route of each run, collected only while you record a run in the app.
  • Events and groups: events you join or apply to, groups you join, and check-in status at events.
  • Community content: messages, reactions and reports you send in community rooms, group chats and direct messages.
  • AI Coach: the messages you send to the coach, and aggregate running totals (see section 5).
  • Device data: a notification token for your device, if you allow notifications.
  • Safety records: moderation decisions, strikes, restrictions and appeals about your account.

3. Location

We use location only when you start a run. We ask for permission at that point. Your route is stored privately and is not shown to other runners. We never share live location. Event meeting points and group areas are public, but we store group areas rounded to a general area rather than an exact address.

4. How we use your data

We use your data to provide the app: sign-in, your profile, runs, streaks, badges, leaderboards, events, groups, chat, notifications, safety and moderation, and to meet legal obligations. Leaderboards use only verified GPS runs and show your display name and distance.

5. AI features and automated screening

AI Coach: you choose whether to share running totals (distance, time and number of runs over the last 30 days) with the coach. Routes and location are never sent. Your questions and the coach's replies are sent to our AI provider, OpenRouter, to produce a response. AI suggestions are general information, not medical advice.

Community screening: messages in community spaces may be checked by automated systems for abuse, spam and personal information. Automated checks can be wrong, so flagged content can be reviewed by a human moderator and appealed. Content flagged for serious risk may be hidden while it's reviewed.

6. Who we share data with

  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), OpenRouter (AI Coach and moderation checks), and Google (sign-in).
  • Event organisers see the details of runners who join or apply to their event: name, age, city, gender, experience and check-in status, to help identify runners at the start.
  • Other runners see your public profile fields you choose to show, your display name on leaderboards, and your name in groups and chats you share.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law or to protect safety.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account and activity while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, routes, runs, messages you sent and registrations within 30 days, except records we must keep for safety or legal reasons, such as moderation records about a suspension, which are kept for up to 12 months. Events you organised remain published, without your name.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete account) or on our web page: delete-account.html. Deletion removes the data described in section 7 and your sign-in.

9. Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, control visibility and friend requests in Privacy and blocking, turn off AI personalisation, and turn off notifications in your phone settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict some processing. To use these rights, contact us at the email above.

10. Children

Pacekin is for people aged [16] and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger people. If you believe a younger person has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit, row-level access rules in our database, and restricted access for staff. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong Google account and tell us if you notice a problem.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will tell you in the app about material changes before they take effect.
